# 1. Concepts

A **delegation** is one bounded assignment identified by `id`. A **delegator** submits it; a **worker** accepts or rejects it; a **runtime** enforces policy and exposes lifecycle operations. These roles need not map one-to-one to processes, models, or providers.

The objective describes desired work, not permission. Context references are caller-selected inputs, never implicit authority. Effective authority is computed before execution. Workspace identity locates the work. Constraints express operational requirements; budgets account for consumable resources. Acceptance criteria define how success is judged. Artifacts are addressable outputs; ordinary output is independent structured data. Diagnostics explain runtime conditions and usage reports accounting.

Parent lineage makes nested work traceable. Internal chain-of-thought and token-by-token reasoning are not ADP data. Coarse progress is operational, concise, and optional.
